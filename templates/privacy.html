Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — that you enter while using the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate and improve Spendly: to authenticate your account, store and display your expense data back to you, and provide features like spending summaries and category breakdowns. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Spendly, our users, or others.

5. Your Choices

You can review, update, or delete the expense data associated with your account at any time through the Service. If you wish to delete your account entirely, you may contact us to request removal of your data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Service or applicable regulations. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
